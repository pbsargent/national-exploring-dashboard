Quick Start

  1. Check the source date. This is a published workbook snapshot. Reloading the website does not refresh the OneDrive workbook.
  2. Start with the national summary. Review membership, unit metrics and focus-area comparisons.
  3. Narrow the view. Combine CST, focus, health, search and a quick filter to find posts for follow-up.
  4. Open the board. Expand a CST, then a council, then select a post to review its detail.

Filters, Search and Expand/Collapse

Search and selectors
Search matches post name, council, district, charter organization or unit number without regard to case. CST means Council Service Territory. All active controls combine.
Quick filters
Growing means primary YOY is positive. Needs attention uses the health rules below. Renewing soon means a renewal falls in the next 120 days from the capture timestamp; past renewals are excluded.
What changes with filters?
The Focus Area Metrics panel, Post Health Board and visible-post counts update. National summary totals and the lower CST, council, focus, renewal and metric charts remain national snapshots.
Expand and Collapse
Use a CST or council row to open or close that group. Expand opens all groups in the current view; Collapse closes them. Scroll the board horizontally on smaller screens.
Post detail
Select a post row for its council, renewal, membership, training, retention and metric signals. The detail stays on the last selected post when filters change.
Return to the full view
Clear Search, select All CSTs, All focus areas and All health, then select the All quick filter.

Common Measures

Posts and youth
Posts counts the workbook's unit rows, including Clubs. National membership totals follow the workbook Dashboard; focus and board youth totals use Posts rows. Primary youth and national primary YOY do not currently reconcile with the Posts rows.
YOY
Year-over-year change in primary youth, shown as a count rather than a percentage. Focus-area YOY sums Posts rows. The youth/YOY pair inside a post row uses the matched UnitMetric YOY when available; the selected post detail uses primary YOY.
Unit Metric
The source UnitMetric score, not a dashboard-created rating. Metric bands are Strong at 4 or higher, Watch at 2 to below 4, and Risk below 2. Missing matched scores currently contribute zero to averages and fall in Risk.
Training
Complete means both Unit Leader (UL) and Committee Chair (CC) are marked trained. Partial means at least one training signal is present; otherwise Gap. The focus-area percentage is the share of posts marked Complete. The national card's metric-credit percentage instead counts scores of at least 1; it is not a training completion rate.
Average retention
An unweighted average of matched UnitMetric retention values between 0% and 100%. Missing and out-of-range values are excluded. Hover over a focus retention value for the number of included posts. N/A means there are no eligible values, not zero retention.
Focus areas
Inferred from charter organization and post name, with the workbook Law/Fire flag as a fallback. These are approximate categories, not an official career-interest classification.
Progress bars
The share of six signals met across the group's posts: size, growth, youth leadership, outdoor, complete UL/CC training, and a Unit Metric of at least 3. Each signal has equal weight.
Renewal labels
Next 120 is a future renewal within 120 days of capture. Past is before capture; Later is beyond the window; Unscheduled has no renewal date. These labels do not prove that a renewal has been submitted or approved.

Health and Status Labels

Strong health
At least 10 youth, nonnegative primary YOY and a Unit Metric of at least 2.
Needs attention
If not Strong: fewer than 5 youth, primary YOY below -5, or a zero/missing Unit Metric. Other posts are Watch.
Board signals
Size, growth, leadership and outdoor come from UnitMetric flags. Green is a positive signal, gold indicates review, and red indicates a gap or risk; text labels give the meaning.
Missing records
Unmatched UnitMetric records currently show zero scores and unmet signals. A missing match is not proof that the unit failed a requirement. Use Source and metric definitions to check known coverage gaps.

Sources, Freshness and Privacy

The public JSON and Posts CSV contain the published snapshot. Contact names, email, phone, address and website fields are excluded. Confirm discrepancies against the original workbook before acting.

Troubleshooting

Missing post or empty board
Clear Search and all filters, including the quick filter. Expand the appropriate CST and council. If the record is still missing, compare with the source workbook.
Old numbers or appearance
Reload the page and check Data as of at the top. If the source workbook is newer, the dashboard needs a new capture and publication.
Totals disagree
Check whether the view is national or filtered, and distinguish total youth from primary youth and metric YOY. Review the source reconciliation notes above.
Need assistance
Include the dashboard URL, source date, selected filters, affected unit ID, expected result and a screenshot with your support request.
